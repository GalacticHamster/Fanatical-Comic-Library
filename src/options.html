Import from Fanatical

Sign in to Fanatical in this browser, open the purchase importer, and select Import new purchases. Use Rescan all purchases to check the complete purchase history for the selected account and update its saved list.

How account nicknames work

A nickname labels a website account locally; it does not detect your login. Use Saved accounts to switch to an existing saved purchase list without a full rescan. Rename account changes only its name and preserves scan history. For another website account, sign in on the website, open Advanced accounts in the importer, select Save a new account, and rescan all purchases once. A nickname is optional. Only the active account is matched; other saved accounts are kept separately.

Account naming is optional. Import new purchases reuses your saved account and checks the complete history on the first import. Use Advanced accounts in the importer when adding another website account.

Wishlist and priorities

Add titles here or use Wishlist in a bundle summary. Priority is your preference from 1 to 100; higher priorities matter more in purchase plans. Wishes do not establish ownership.

What happens after I add a wish?

When you open a supported bundle, the extension compares its items with your saved wishes. Choose the Wishlist purchase goal to see the lowest tier or selection that covers eligible wishes and the priority available at each price. Owned items are excluded; uncertain matches are excluded unless you include them. A wish may have no eligible target in the bundle you opened.

The extension does not monitor new bundles or send wishlist notifications. Wishlist entries and priorities are local to this extension and are not shared with the other extension or Galactic Library.

    Galactic Library connection

    Purchase sync

    Check other libraries: When you view a bundle, Galactic Library helps show which titles you own elsewhere and which may be matches. After connecting, refresh an already-open bundle page to update its results.

    Sync purchases: Choose Connect and sync to share saved purchases between the two extensions. Saved purchases synchronize automatically in both directions; no store rescan is needed. Manual recovery stays under Advanced.

    Check connection verifies communication; it does not turn on automatic sync. Checking other libraries works separately from syncing saved purchases.

          Privacy

          Your library, wishlist and preferences stay in this browser. Galactic Library linking exchanges only the match or reminder evidence needed for the action you request; supported provider purchases are shared with your authorized companion after you enable automatic sync, or explicitly approve a manual transfer. Disconnect stops future automatic sharing and keeps existing purchases.

          Purchase metadata is stored only in this browser. The importer reads Fanatical's session token only in the Fanatical page context; neither tokens nor raw order responses are stored, exported, or sent to another extension. Quick scans retain only SHA-256 fingerprints of completed-order IDs, never the IDs themselves.
