Fanatical Library

Your library stays in this browser. Import directly from your signed-in Fanatical purchase history or restore a JSON backup.

Import from Fanatical

Sign in to Fanatical in this browser, open the purchase importer, and select Import new purchases. Use Rescan all purchases to rebuild the active account's snapshot from the full purchase history.

How account nicknames work

An account nickname is a name you choose for the website account you import, such as personal. It is stored only in this browser; it does not sign you in or detect your login. Only one account snapshot is active for ownership checks. Sign in to the other account on the website, enter its nickname, confirm it, select Replace active account, and use Rescan all purchases to switch. A successful full rescan archives the previous snapshot for backup recovery; archived accounts are not combined with the active library.

Not yet named means no account nickname has been established. Confirm a nickname in the importer and run a complete rescan to establish it. The first import automatically requires a full scan.

Wishlist and priorities

Add titles here or use Wishlist in a bundle summary. Priority is your preference from 1 to 100; higher priorities matter more in purchase plans. Wishes do not establish ownership.

What happens after I add a wish?

When you open a supported bundle, the extension compares its items with your saved wishes. Choose the Wishlist purchase goal to see the lowest tier or selection that covers eligible wishes and the priority available at each price. Owned items are excluded; uncertain matches are excluded unless you include them. A wish may have no eligible target in the bundle you opened.

The extension does not monitor new bundles or send wishlist notifications. Wishlist entries and priorities are local to this extension and are not shared with the other extension or Galactic Library.

        Privacy

        Your library, wishlist and preferences stay in this browser. Galactic Library linking exchanges only the match or reminder evidence needed for the action you request; neither full library is transferred.

        Purchase metadata is stored only in this browser. The importer reads Fanatical's session token only in the Fanatical page context; neither tokens nor raw order responses are stored, exported, or sent to another extension. Quick scans retain only SHA-256 fingerprints of completed-order IDs, never the IDs themselves.
